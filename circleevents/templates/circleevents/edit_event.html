To improve approval chances, make your listing clear, specific, and inclusive:

  • Provide complete accessibility details, including step-free access, toilets, seating, and quiet spaces.
  • Use fair, respectful language and avoid discriminatory wording in event descriptions.
  • Add practical details people can trust: exact venue, timings, contact email, and ticket information.
  • Use the access and sensory note fields to help people decide if the event is suitable for their needs.

Events that are transparent, accessible, and community-focused are more likely to be approved quickly.

Open Circle expects events to be safe, welcoming, and respectful for everyone.

  • Do not tolerate harassment, hate speech, bullying, or discrimination.
  • State any behaviour expectations and boundaries clearly in your event information.
  • Explain how attendees can report concerns and who to contact during the event.
  • Consider accessibility, consent, and safeguarding when planning activities and spaces.

If your event includes clear safe-space expectations, it helps moderators and attendees trust your listing.
