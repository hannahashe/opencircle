Our Values

Accessibility First

Events presented with clarity for physical, sensory and community access needs. Organisers can submit detailed accessibility information, and our moderators review all events to ensure they meet our standards.

Community Led

Built for and by queer and alternative communities. Everyone can submit events, which are reviewed by our moderators. Sign up is easy and free, and organisers can manage their submissions in their user profile.

Safer Spaces

Transparent safer-space policies and moderation standards. Events that don't meet our standards won't be published, and organisers will receive feedback to help them improve their submissions.
